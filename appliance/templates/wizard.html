Each selected version is compressed, retrained with simulated low-precision weights (quantization-aware training, or QAT), compressed again, then evaluated. More updates take longer and do not guarantee better quality. Expert removal is optional; leave it off for your first comparison.

Choose how to measure success

Automatic checks: held-out loss, packed export parity, checkpoint restart, and measured GPU memory. Full context and published coding suites are outside this synthetic proof.
